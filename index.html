How to Use This Calendar

  • Daily Saving Amount: Save the amount corresponding to the day number (Day 1 = 1 Ksh, Day 365 = 365 Ksh)
  • Total Annual Savings: You will save Ksh 66,795 by the end of the year!
  • How to track: Click on any day number in the monthly sections below to mark it as "saved" when you've deposited that day's amount
  • Start Date: You can start anytime! Day 1 is whenever you begin your challenge
  • Monthly Tracking: Track your progress month by month with visual indicators
  • Print: Use the Print button to get a physical copy for offline tracking
